# 📊 Target Retail Sales Performance Dashboard (Q1 2025)

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Retail Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze key business metrics, customer behavior, product performance, delivery efficiency, and regional sales trends.

The dashboard provides decision-makers with a consolidated view of retail operations and customer insights through dynamic visualizations and interactive filtering.

- Top-selling products analysis

### Regional Sales Distribution
- Geographic sales visualization

### Delivery Performance

## Business Insights

- App-based purchases accounted for the largest share of customer orders.
- T-Shirts and Jeans emerged as the highest-performing product categories.
- Sales were concentrated in key California regions.
- Most deliveries were completed within 1–3 days.
- Customer satisfaction remained consistently high throughout Q1 2025.
